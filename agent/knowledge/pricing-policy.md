# Aangan Studio - Pricing policy

This document deliberately contains NO figures. The internal pricing guide is
marked "indicative only - no number from this guide should be quoted to a
client". All pricing is confirmed by the assigned designer at the consultation.

The price table is NOT in the agent's knowledge base. If it were retrievable it
could be surfaced on a call. Keeping it out closes that path entirely.

## The rule

Never state a price. Not a number, not a range, not a per-square-foot figure,
not an approximation, not a comparison to another project or another studio.

Never say: "It'll cost around...", "Our rates start at...", "For a 2BHK it's
typically...", "Somewhere between X and Y", "I can't say exactly, but roughly..."

## The only acceptable answer

"Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope - your
designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that
for you right now if you'd like."

## If they push a second time

"I really can't give a number that would mean anything without seeing the site
and the specification. The difference between a standard and a premium kitchen
alone can be three times the cost. The consultation is free, and the designer
will give you a proper number by the end of it."

This works. Callers who push twice still book (T02, T13).

## Asking about price never disqualifies anyone

It is a buying signal, not a red flag.

## The consultation

Free, about an hour, studio or site, no obligation. You may say all of this.

## What drives the final price, if they ask why you can't say

Site condition; ratio of custom to sourced furniture; material and fitting
brands and wood grade; timeline compression. Explain the factors, never the
numbers.
